Roadmap to the Platinum

About 200 hours in total, difficulty 4 / 10 (PowerPyx). One playthrough is enough: there is no mission replay, but everything outside the story stays open after the credits. Work the stages in order.

Stage totals come from the official descriptions and the launch-week guides: the story is five chapters and takes 60–80 hours; the 100% checklist is the long middle at about 100 hours; side stories and cleanup take about 10 hours.

Missables — what can actually go wrong

Only one trophy is reported as potentially missable. Everything else stays available after the credits. The danger is not a trophy — it is the lack of mission replay, so your saves are the safety net.

Save like there is no replay — because there is not

GTA VI has no mission replay and no New Game Plus. The game gives you 15 manual save slots plus one autosave. Keep one save at the start of every chapter, one untouched save from just before the final mission, and rotate the rest. That is the whole insurance policy: with those saves, a missed meditation or a failed time-sensitive race costs you a reload, not a second playthrough.

Race Against Yago is time-sensitive

This Chapter 3 race in Yago's street-racing strand carries an hourglass icon: it disappears if you leave it too long, and the strand counts toward Strangers & Freaks. Save first, then race the moment it appears.

Leave cheats off on your trophy save

Using cheats should switch off both trophies and saving. Cheats only unlock after the story anyway, so there is no reason to touch them before the Platinum pops.

What is reported, not yet verified in-game

Most methods in this guide come from the launch-week guides (PowerPyx, Output Lag, KeenGamer, GTA Zone), written from the game on launch day. They agree with each other on the big facts — the 200-hour estimate, the 4/10 difficulty, the single missable — but a launch-day guide is still early guidance, and the details may move as more players play. Where a method is only reported, the guide says so.
