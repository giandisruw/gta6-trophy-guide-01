Roadmap to the Platinum — about 200 hours, one playthrough

No online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals, no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Everything outside the story stays available in free-roam after the credits. Keep one manual save at the start of every chapter and one untouched save from just before the final mission — the game has 15 manual save slots plus one autosave.

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story, Chapters 1–5 60–80 hours

    Finish all five chapters and the named story missions. Along the way: do Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1, do Race Against Yago the moment it appears in Chapter 3, and take optional missions as they appear (black icons with pink letters) — most of them do not matter for trophies, but the ones below do.

    • Stage 2 — The 100% completion checklist about 100 hours

      After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Progress made during the story already counts, and nothing on the checklist is missable. PowerPyx notes the checklist is roughly 60% of true completion — lenient, but long.

      • A Long Road (gold): finish the 100% checklist — 300 stunts, 70 of the 80 Challenges, 200 of the 277 base-model vehicles scanned, 100 of the 255 animals studied through binoculars, all 8 bank vaults, all 39 gang operations, 15 of the 18 meditation spots.
      • Cunning Stunts: 250 stunts for the trophy, 300 for the checklist — keep going past 250.
      • One Last Thing: 50 Challenges for the trophy, 70 of 80 for the checklist.
      • Whip It Good + You WAiNKer!: scan vehicles and WAiNK locations as you travel; 200 of 277 vehicles and 100 locations feed the checklist too.
      • Maximum Withdrawal: all 8 bank vaults (the trophy needs 5); Pest Control: all 39 gang operations (the trophy needs one per gang).

    • Stage 3 — Side stories and cleanup about 10 hours

      Finish the six side-story finales (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret), the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands, the card sets and the remaining feats. Then the Platinum pops.

      Difficulty 4/10 and the ~200-hour estimate are from PowerPyx’s trophy guide (18 Nov 2026). The hardest trophy by every published guide is A Long Road — the 100% checklist.

      Missable trophies

      Good news first: only one trophy can be missed, and only possibly. Every other trophy — story included — is earned by playing, and everything outside the story stays available in free-roam after the credits. There is no mission replay, so the safety net is manual saves, not replays.

      ⚠ Potentially missable Positive Mental Attitude Bronze

      Meditate at every spot across Leonida — 18 spots in 10 locations. The first meditation is Lucia’s, on the balcony of Jason’s house in Chapter 1: after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and choose “Accept”. It is gone once Chapter 1 ends. It is not confirmed that the trophy strictly needs that spot — PowerPyx earned the trophy after doing it — so do it anyway and keep a Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops.

      Meditation spots show as purple icons when you are close. Each outdoor spot should count twice if you meditate there with both characters, so bring your partner every time. The 100% checklist only needs 15 of the 18 spots, so this trophy is the only reason the Chapter 1 meditation matters.

      Sources: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap; Output Lag — GTA 6 Trophy List. PowerPyx guide · Output Lag list

      Time-sensitive, but not trophy-missable

      • Race Against Yago (Chapter 3, Yago’s street racing strand, hourglass icon) — do it the moment it appears. It counts toward Strangers & Freaks; missing the race can cost you that strand.
      • Optional missions (black icons with pink letters) disappear after a while, but that does not matter for trophies — except the two above.

      Everything else is safe

      • All 12 unmissable story trophies pop as you play; chapter trophies can pop at the start of the next chapter.
      • Side-story finales (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) stay open after the story. Andy’s strand is Lucia-only; Tyrone’s and Bret’s are Jason-only.
      • Never use cheats on your trophy save — cheats are reported to switch off trophies and saving. Use a throwaway save if you try them.
